History

Where ACEIoT comes from.

ACEIoT was established as part of the African Centres of Excellence initiative, funded by the World Bank and the Government of Rwanda. The centre is hosted at the University of Rwanda's College of Science and Technology, in Kigali, and was formally launched to address the growing demand for advanced ICT expertise in East and Central Africa.

The Centre's mandate is to provide postgraduate education and applied research in Internet of Things, data science, AI, and digital infrastructure — with a particular focus on the practical needs of communities across the region.

Since its founding, ACEIoT has graduated over 200 specialists, published more than 400 peer-reviewed outputs, and contributed to digital transformation projects across health, agriculture, and urban infrastructure sectors.

Timeline

Key milestones.

  1. 2016

    Centre established

    ACEIoT officially opens at the University of Rwanda with World Bank and government support.

  2. 2018

    First cohort graduates

    24 students complete MSc programs in IoT and Embedded Systems — the first from the region.

  3. 2020

    PhD program launched

    Doctoral program in ICT opens, attracting researchers from across Africa.

  4. 2022

    Phase II funding secured

    Continued investment supports expanded labs, new research partnerships, and executive programmes.

  5. 2025

    Expanding regional reach

    ACEIoT deepens collaboration across East African universities and launches new open research initiatives.
